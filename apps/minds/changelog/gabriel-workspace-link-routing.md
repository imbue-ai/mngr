A link or popup a workspace page opens (`target="_blank"`, `window.open`) to a local address or one of the workspace's app addresses now opens inside the workspace, on workspace templates that announce they handle it: a local URL in the in-workspace browser, an app address in that app's own window. Such a popup no longer opens a bare Imbue Studio window. Workspaces on older templates keep today's window, and external links still open in the default browser.

Under the hood: the embed contract moves to version 7 with `minds:open-link {url}` (embedder to workspace) and an optional `opensLinks` field on `minds:workspace-ready`, and its compatibility rule now allows adding optional fields to a shipped type. The Electron popup decision moves to `electron/link-routing.js`; each window's page reports its mounted workspace and whether it announced `opensLinks` to main, which denies that workspace's local popups and hands their URLs back to send as `minds:open-link`. `electron/link-externality-vectors.json` pins which URLs Electron treats as external, for the workspace template's link classifier to agree with.
