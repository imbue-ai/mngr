When the background carry of a remote workspace's registered agents to its machine gives up, it logs one error with the failure attached as an exception, instead of one of two per-step messages with the failure flattened into their text. The log record and its Sentry event hold the failure's type, cause chain and traceback, including the failed machine command with what it was sending and what it expected back. Sentry groups these errors by the failure rather than by message text. A carry cut short by the app shutting down while it waits to retry reports the shutdown as what lost the change; the transient failure it was waiting out is logged before the wait. The retry policy is unchanged (two retries, after 10s and then 30s, for transient failures only) and now runs on tenacity.

A failed change to a workspace's machine (a grant, a sign-out, a permissions edit) no longer prefixes its technical detail with a description of the action. `MachineOperationError.transfer` names the failed machine command, or is `None` when no command is what failed. The sentence shown to the user is unchanged.
