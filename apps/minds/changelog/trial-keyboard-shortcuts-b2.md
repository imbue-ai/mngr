Imbue Studio's own shortcuts are rebindable: New window (Cmd+N / Ctrl+N), Close window (Cmd+W / Ctrl+W), Quit (Cmd+Q / Ctrl+Q) and Zoom in, Zoom out and Reset zoom (Cmd+= , Cmd+- , Cmd+0, or Ctrl elsewhere, where the zoom keys are new). They are the last section of the Keyboard shortcuts group in Workspace settings, in the desktop app only, and are saved to the account (`keybindings["imbue-studio"]`). Main runs them before any page or the OS sees the key; the macOS menu shows the chosen keys and its items still work when a shortcut is Off. Zoom is the Settings > Display size, so it applies to every window and persists. On Windows and Linux the workspace switcher menu gains New window and Zoom entries.

The account-settings route (`/ui/api/settings/<account_id>`) reads and writes these shortcuts, merging per action and accepting no other app; a workspace's "everywhere" keybindings save keeps them rather than replacing them from its snapshot. Embed contract v16 adds `minds:chrome-shortcuts` and `minds:save-chrome-shortcut` for the framed shortcuts page.

While the shortcuts page records a shortcut, main hands it every key, so a key being recorded (Cmd+Q, Cmd+W, Cmd+H, the developer-tools key and the rest) does nothing else.

An action holds at most 16 shortcuts (`MAX_CHORDS_PER_ACTION`, as the embed contract carries): both keybindings write paths refuse more. A stored chord no binding can use, such as Ctrl+Alt+Del saved before it was refused, no longer blocks a save: the account-settings PUT and a workspace's "everywhere" save drop it, and Imbue Studio's own shortcuts are served without it.
