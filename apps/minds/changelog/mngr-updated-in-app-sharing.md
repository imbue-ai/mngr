The home page is redesigned (`specs/home-screen-redesign/spec.md`) as three tabs. Workspaces lists the account's own workspaces as cards painted with each workspace's wallpaper (read through the forward when the workspace is loaded and cached under `<data_dir>/wallpapers/`, served by `GET /ui/api/workspaces/<id>/wallpaper`; a workspace never loaded here, or on its theme's default, is a tile of its accent), with one run control, Open in new window, a "..." menu holding where the workspace runs, Backups, and Workspace settings, and every status as a badge in one of three tones (red for broken, orange for needs you, a white chip for information and progress), the grid ending in an "Add workspace" card and the "Recently destroyed workspaces" link under it; below them, the workspaces other people shared with the account as guest cards naming the owner, with liveness and the last open's verdict as badges and open, retry, decline, report, and remove in the menu, and the pasted-link input. Apps lists the apps of the account's live workspaces (carried on the UI channel as each entry's `apps`, their icons served by `GET /ui/api/workspaces/<id>/apps/<name>/icon.svg`, a monogram standing in for an app without one) as desktop icons that open the workspace on that app, then the apps shared with the account (one icon per target of a share without a shell target, and one per app of a shared workspace that the owner granted in its own right, the sharer's face on the tile; a pasted link is an app when its label is not the shell's), with its own pasted-link input for an app link someone gave you. Invitations, shown while any listed share is unanswered or declined, lists each as a card to Join (which opens it) or Decline (the card stays with Undo until the tab is left, then files under a collapsed "Declined invitations"). The chosen tab is remembered per window, the invitation deep link lands on its share's tab, "Studio settings" moves into the section head, the bottom-left launchers go, and the account switcher's menu gains "Account settings". In the default-workspace-template, Files and Routines are now system apps, so they are not listed among a workspace's own apps (a workspace that predates the shell's share-kind announcement sorts them the same way).

The recipient's side of sharing (`specs/shared-with-me/spec.md`): every workspace other people shared with the window's account is listed with the owner, the status (invitation, accepted, declined), liveness, the last open's verdict, and an open per target, plus decline (and undo), report (a strike against the owner that cannot be withdrawn, after a confirmation), retry, remove, and a pasted-link input. Opening a share navigates the workspace frame to the reverse device handoff's redeem page, which signs the window's per-account Electron session partition in on the accounts origin, confirms the account for the share, and sends the frame on to the share's own URL; the shell draws a connecting overlay over the frame until the workspace's page is up (with Retry once the open runs long or fails) and the card reads "Connecting…" meanwhile, drops only the `frame-ancestors` directive from the responses of a listed share's or a pasted link's hosts and from the accounts origin's pages shown in that frame, records what the frame answered as the card's verdict (including a sign-in that did not carry the frame onto the share), and never leaves the accounts origin's sign-in form in the frame. A workspace whose create is still running is listed under the account it was created for, rather than the device, until its association lands. The `imbue-studio://shared/<host-id>` deep link (the invitation email's "Open it in the app" link) refreshes every signed-in account's listing at once and lands on the card, or on the home page with a note saying the workspace is not shared with any account here, or that the listings could not be checked.

A shared workspace reports `is_owned=false` on the UI channel (schema 29), on `GET /api/v1/workspaces` (with its `share_link`), and in the titlebar, which hides the owner-only tabs; its shell is told it can neither pull a window out nor manage local files; every owner action named at a shared workspace is refused with `403 not_owned` (Report a bug over one files the device's own report, with no agent to ask), a shared row with no address yet loads nothing rather than a local forward, and a window opening on a shared workspace's page takes up the account it was shared with (the owner's own, when this device also hosts the workspace for another signed-in account). The desktop caches each account's listing under `<data_dir>/shared_with_me/` and refreshes it from the sync pass when the match set the records pull carries differs from the cached listing's (a connector that does not serve the listing yet reads as nothing shared); verdicts and pasted links live in a `shared_with_me` account document.

Each per-account Electron partition is prepared on first use with what the default session already had: the permission policy (including the ask-first routing of Chromium's `openExternal` permission through the link router), the forward proxy's CA, the forward session cookie, and the backend session cookie the one-time login code buys on the default session, which the windows' pages carry from their partitions.

On the owner's side, a grants push now carries each target's label and display name and the workspace name (the targets only as a complete set, so a push made before every app has registered keeps the connector's recorded links), and a domain grant may only name the granter's own email domain (`grantable_email_domain` in the options payload, empty for a granter at a public provider, who may grant none; the share panel refuses either before the row as the save does, and a refusal the connector answers to a push is shown the same way).
